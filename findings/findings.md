# Security Findings

## Target

Target: localhost

## Tools Used

- Nmap
- ping
- nslookup
- dig

## Findings

### Finding 1 — Network Connectivity

The localhost system responded successfully to ICMP ping requests.

### Finding 2 — Open Ports

Nmap was used to identify ports exposed by the local system.

### Finding 3 — Service Detection

Nmap service detection was used to identify services associated with discovered ports.

### Finding 4 — DNS Information

DNS queries were performed to understand how domain names resolve to IP addresses.

## Scope

All active scanning in this lab was performed against systems under my control or explicitly authorized lab targets.
